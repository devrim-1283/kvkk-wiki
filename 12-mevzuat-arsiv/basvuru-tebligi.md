## Article-by-Article Summary

### ARTICLE 1 — Purpose

| Field | Content |
|-------|---------|
| Summary | To set the procedures and principles for application to the controller, and any cost-based fee |
| Operational Impact | Normative framework for the data subject application process |
| Wiki | [09-ilgili-kisi-basvurulari](../09-ilgili-kisi-basvurulari/) |

### ARTICLE 2 — Legal Basis

| Field | Content |
|-------|---------|
| Summary | KVKK Arts. 13 and 22/1-(e) and (g) |

### ARTICLE 3 — Definitions

| Field | Content |
|-------|---------|
| Summary | Definitions of applicant, registered electronic mail (KEP), data subject, KVKK, controller, mobile/secure electronic signature |

### ARTICLE 4 — Right to Apply

| Field | Content |
|-------|---------|
| Summary | Natural persons whose personal data are processed have the right to apply to the controller; can exercise rights under Art. 11; application is free of charge, unless the operation entails cost in which case the Authority's tariff applies |
| Operational Impact | The right belongs to natural persons (applications by legal persons are out of scope) |
| Wiki | [09-ilgili-kisi-basvurulari](../09-ilgili-kisi-basvurulari/) |

### ARTICLE 5 — Application Procedure

| Field | Content |
|-------|---------|
| Summary | The data subject submits Art. 11 requests: (a) in writing; (b) via KEP address; (c) with secure electronic signature; (ç) with mobile signature; (d) via the email **previously notified to the controller and registered in its system**; (e) via a software/application designed for the purpose. The application contains **name-surname and signature, ID number (foreigners: nationality + passport/ID), service address, email/phone if any, request subject**. Supporting documents are attached if any |
| Operational Impact | Web application form must map exactly to the Communiqué fields; KEP address must be publicly notified; "registered email" method requires that the customer/employee email database is in sync |
| Wiki | [09-ilgili-kisi-basvurulari](../09-ilgili-kisi-basvurulari/), [99-sablonlar/ilgili-kisi-cevap.md](../99-sablonlar/ilgili-kisi-cevap.md) |

### ARTICLE 6 — Controller Obligations

| Field | Content |
|-------|---------|
| Summary | The controller resolves applications **effectively, lawfully and in accordance with good-faith principles**; takes administrative and technical measures. Within **30 days** of receipt the application is concluded. Response is given in writing or electronically. (a) If accepted, the request is performed and notified; (b) if rejected, **reasons are explained** and notified in writing or electronically |
| Operational Impact | 30 days — KPI ceiling (15 days target, 30 days max); refusals must be reasoned; SLA discipline |
| Wiki | [09-ilgili-kisi-basvurulari](../09-ilgili-kisi-basvurulari/), [11-denetim-ve-uyum/kpi-ve-metrikler.md](../11-denetim-ve-uyum/kpi-ve-metrikler.md) |

### ARTICLE 7 — Fee

| Field | Content |
|-------|---------|
| Summary | (1) Written response **up to ten pages free of charge**; **TRY 1 per page** beyond ten pages. (2) If response is delivered on CD, USB or similar medium, the controller may charge **up to cost** |
| Operational Impact | Standard tariff; due to small amounts, free-response policy generally applied; CD/USB at cost |
| Wiki | [09-ilgili-kisi-basvurulari](../09-ilgili-kisi-basvurulari/) |

### ARTICLE 8 — Entry into Force

| Field | Content |
|-------|---------|
| Summary | Entered into force on the date of publication (10.03.2018) |

### ARTICLE 9 — Execution

| Field | Content |
|-------|---------|
| Summary | The President of the Personal Data Protection Authority executes the Communiqué |

## Operational Checklist

- [ ] Web request form ready (all fields per Communiqué Art. 5)
- [ ] KEP address publicly disclosed and monitored
- [ ] Registered email database in sync for the "registered email" channel
- [ ] Mobile/secure-electronic-signature applications accepted
- [ ] 30-day SLA automated in the ticket system
- [ ] Acceptance / rejection templates with reasoning (Art. 6)
- [ ] Fee tariff defined (10 pages free; TRY 1/page above; CD/USB at cost)
- [ ] Monthly application report to the KVKK Committee

## Note on Identity Verification

Identity verification is not strictly mandatory under the Communiqué but is a reasonable safeguard against applications using third-party data:
- Wet-ink + signature: ID copy (only the necessary fields per the proportionality principle of KVKK Art. 4)
- KEP / e-signature: identity is already verified
- "Registered email": reasonable verification, no additional document
- Application on behalf of a third party: power of attorney

## Kimlik Doğrulama Notu

İlgili kişinin kimlik doğrulaması Tebliğ kapsamında zorunlu olmamakla birlikte, başkasının verileri ile başvuruyu engellemek için makul önlem niteliğindedir:
- Yazılı + ıslak imza: kimlik fotokopisi (KVKK m.4 ölçülülük gereği yalnızca gerekli alanlar)
- KEP / e-imza: kimlik zaten doğrulanmıştır
- "Sistemde kayıtlı e-posta": doğrulama makul, ek belge gereksiz
- Üçüncü kişi adına başvuru: vekaletname
