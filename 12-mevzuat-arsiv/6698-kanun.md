## Article-by-Article Summary

### ARTICLE 1 — Purpose

| Field | Content |
|-------|---------|
| Summary | To protect fundamental rights and freedoms of persons (in particular the privacy of private life) in the processing of personal data, and to set the obligations of natural and legal persons processing personal data |
| Operational Impact | The overarching purpose of the entire KVKK compliance framework; cited in the "purpose" sections of policies |
| Wiki | [00-yonetisim](../00-yonetisim/) |

### ARTICLE 2 — Scope

| Field | Content |
|-------|---------|
| Summary | Applies to natural persons whose personal data are processed and to natural and legal persons processing personal data wholly or partly through automated means or, if part of a data filing system, through non-automated means |
| Operational Impact | The Company is a legal-person controller; all departments are within scope; manual records (e.g. paper CVs) are within scope if part of a data filing system |
| Wiki | [01-temel-kavramlar](../01-temel-kavramlar/) |

### ARTICLE 3 — Definitions

| Field | Content |
|-------|---------|
| Summary | Definitions of explicit consent, anonymisation, data subject, personal data, processing, Authority, Institution, processor, data filing system, controller, erasure (deletion/destruction), Registry |
| Operational Impact | Foundation for term usage across all documents; in particular, the controller / processor distinction is critical for contractual status |
| Wiki | [01-temel-kavramlar](../01-temel-kavramlar/) |

### ARTICLE 4 — General Principles

| Field | Content |
|-------|---------|
| Summary | Five mandatory principles for processing: (a) lawfulness and fairness, (b) accuracy and where necessary up-to-date, (c) specific, explicit and legitimate purpose, (ç) relevant, limited and proportionate to the processing purpose, (d) retained for the period required by relevant legislation or for the purpose |
| Operational Impact | Apply as a checklist in all process design; foundational in DPIA, consent and disclosure texts; data minimisation (sub-paragraph ç) must be considered at product development (privacy by design) |
| Wiki | [01-temel-kavramlar](../01-temel-kavramlar/), [10-ozel-konular](../10-ozel-konular/) |

### ARTICLE 5 — Conditions of Processing

| Field | Content |
|-------|---------|
| Summary | Conditions other than explicit consent (Art. 5/2): (a) provided by law, (b) vital interest, (c) conclusion/performance of a contract, (ç) legal obligation, (d) made public by the data subject, (e) establishment, exercise or protection of a right, (f) legitimate interest (without harm to fundamental rights and freedoms) |
| Operational Impact | A "legal basis" field is mandatory in the process inventory for every processing; explicit consent is the last resort; for legitimate interest, an LIA is performed |
| Wiki | [03-aydinlatma-ve-acik-riza](../03-aydinlatma-ve-acik-riza/), [99-sablonlar/meşru-menfaat-degerlendirmesi.md](../99-sablonlar/meşru-menfaat-degerlendirmesi.md) |

### ARTICLE 6 — Processing of Sensitive Personal Data (post 12.03.2024 / Law 7499)

| Field | Content |
|-------|---------|
| Summary | Sensitive data: race, ethnic origin, political opinion, philosophical belief, religion, sect, dress, association/foundation/union membership, health, sexual life, criminal conviction, biometric, genetic. Following Law 7499, multiple legal bases aligned with Art. 5/2 were introduced (formerly explicit consent and a limited list of exceptions). Health and sexual life data still subject to processing by persons under a duty of confidentiality / authorised institution |
| Operational Impact | Adequate measures under Authority Decision 2018/10 mandatory; employee health data, biometric time-attendance, customer ID scans fall in scope |
| Wiki | [10-ozel-konular](../10-ozel-konular/), [05-teknik-tedbirler](../05-teknik-tedbirler/), [12-mevzuat-arsiv/kurul-kararlari-ozeti.md](kurul-kararlari-ozeti.md) |

### ARTICLE 7 — Erasure, Destruction or Anonymisation

| Field | Content |
|-------|---------|
| Summary | Where the reasons for processing have ceased, data are erased, destroyed or anonymised ex officio or upon request. Procedures are governed by the Erasure Regulation |
| Operational Impact | Retention and Destruction Policy is mandatory; periodic destruction calendar (every 6 months); destruction minutes and certificates |
| Wiki | [04-veri-saklama-ve-imha](../04-veri-saklama-ve-imha/), [12-mevzuat-arsiv/imha-yonetmeligi.md](imha-yonetmeligi.md) |

### ARTICLE 8 — Domestic Transfer

| Field | Content |
|-------|---------|
| Summary | Personal data cannot be transferred without the data subject's explicit consent; if conditions of Arts. 5/2 and 6/3 apply, transfer is possible without consent |
| Operational Impact | Data Processing Agreements (DPAs) are signed with domestic suppliers; in joint-controller scenarios, allocation of authority and liability is set out in contract |
| Wiki | [07-aktarim](../07-aktarim/), [99-sablonlar/tedarikci-veri-isleyen-sozlesmesi.md](../99-sablonlar/tedarikci-veri-isleyen-sozlesmesi.md) |

### ARTICLE 9 — Cross-Border Transfer (post 12.03.2024 / Law 7499)

| Field | Content |
|-------|---------|
| Summary | Three-tier system: (1) transfer to countries with adequate protection — designated by the Authority; (2) absent adequacy, transfer based on **standard contracts**, **binding corporate rules (BCR)** or **undertaking** — Authority approval or notification required; (3) **derogations**: explicit consent, performance of contract, public interest, vital interests, etc. (Art. 9/6). Standard contracts must be notified to the Authority within 5 business days of signature |
| Operational Impact | TIA mandatory for every cross-border transfer; standard contract texts published under Authority Decision 2024/959; documentation critical at each tier |
| Wiki | [07-aktarim](../07-aktarim/), [12-mevzuat-arsiv/kurul-kararlari-ozeti.md](kurul-kararlari-ozeti.md) |

### ARTICLE 10 — Disclosure Obligation

| Field | Content |
|-------|---------|
| Summary | At collection, the data subject is informed of: the controller's identity, processing purpose, purpose/recipient of transfer, collection method/legal basis, the rights under Art. 11 |
| Operational Impact | Privacy notices must exist for every channel/process; must contain the minimum elements set out in Art. 4 of the Disclosure Communiqué |
| Wiki | [03-aydinlatma-ve-acik-riza](../03-aydinlatma-ve-acik-riza/), [12-mevzuat-arsiv/aydinlatma-tebligi.md](aydinlatma-tebligi.md), [99-sablonlar/aydinlatma-metni.md](../99-sablonlar/aydinlatma-metni.md) |

### ARTICLE 11 — Rights of the Data Subject

| Field | Content |
|-------|---------|
| Summary | (a) Right to information, (b) to learn whether data are processed, (c) to learn the processing purpose, (ç) to learn the third parties to whom data are transferred domestically/abroad, (d) right of correction in case of incomplete/wrong processing, (e) erasure/destruction, (f) request that correction/erasure/destruction be communicated to third parties, (g) right to object to a result against them produced solely by automated systems, (ğ) right to compensation in case of damage |
| Operational Impact | 30-day SLA for the application process; separate response template for each right; sub-paragraph (g) critical for profiling processes |
| Wiki | [09-ilgili-kisi-basvurulari](../09-ilgili-kisi-basvurulari/), [99-sablonlar/ilgili-kisi-cevap.md](../99-sablonlar/ilgili-kisi-cevap.md) |

### ARTICLE 12 — Data Security Obligations

| Field | Content |
|-------|---------|
| Summary | (1) The controller takes all technical and administrative measures to ensure the appropriate level of security. (2) Joint and several liability with the processor. (3) The controller conducts/causes to conduct audits. (4) Confidentiality continues even after departure. (5) **Breach must be notified to the Authority "as soon as possible"** (set as 72 hours by Authority Decision 2019/02) |
| Operational Impact | Personal Data Security Guide as framework; technical control catalogue; 72-hour clock for breach management; confidentiality undertaking from every employee/contractor/intern |
| Wiki | [05-teknik-tedbirler](../05-teknik-tedbirler/), [06-idari-tedbirler](../06-idari-tedbirler/), [08-ihlal-yonetimi](../08-ihlal-yonetimi/) |

### ARTICLE 13 — Application to the Controller

| Field | Content |
|-------|---------|
| Summary | (1) The data subject applies to the controller in writing or by means determined by the Authority. (2) The controller responds free of charge within **30 days at the latest**; where the operation entails additional cost, the Authority's tariff applies. (3) If denied or response is unsatisfactory/late, the data subject may apply to the Authority |
| Operational Impact | Ticket system, identity verification, fee tariff (Application Communiqué Art. 7); refusals must be reasoned |
| Wiki | [09-ilgili-kisi-basvurulari](../09-ilgili-kisi-basvurulari/), [12-mevzuat-arsiv/basvuru-tebligi.md](basvuru-tebligi.md) |

### ARTICLE 14 — Complaint to the Authority

| Field | Content |
|-------|---------|
| Summary | (1) Where applications are rejected or not answered within 30 days, the data subject may complain to the Authority within 30 days of learning, and in any case within 60 days of application. (2) Right of compensation reserved; suit under general provisions |
| Operational Impact | Tracking of response time is critical (must respond within 60 days); quality and time management to mitigate Authority complaint risk |
| Wiki | [09-ilgili-kisi-basvurulari](../09-ilgili-kisi-basvurulari/) |

### ARTICLE 15 — Inspection upon Complaint or Ex Officio

| Field | Content |
|-------|---------|
| Summary | (1) The Authority inspects upon complaint or ex officio. (3) May request any non-State-secret information/document. (5) Where breach is found, the decision is served for performance **within 30 days**. (7) In irreparable cases, **suspension of processing or cross-border transfer** may be ordered |
| Operational Impact | 15 days to reply to Authority letters; 30 days to comply with breach decisions; suspension order has immediate effect, business continuity plan needed |
| Wiki | [11-denetim-ve-uyum/kurul-denetim-hazirlik.md](../11-denetim-ve-uyum/kurul-denetim-hazirlik.md) |

### ARTICLE 16 — Data Controllers Registry (VERBİS)

| Field | Content |
|-------|---------|
| Summary | (1) The Registry is maintained at the Institution. (2) Registration is required before processing begins. (3) Information to be provided in the application. (4) The Registry is publicly accessible. (5) Governed by regulation |
| Operational Impact | VERBİS registration mandatory; changes must be notified within 7 days; consistency between inventory and VERBİS critical |
| Wiki | [02-envanter-ve-sicil](../02-envanter-ve-sicil/), [12-mevzuat-arsiv/verbis-yonetmelik.md](verbis-yonetmelik.md) |

### ARTICLE 17 — Offences

| Field | Content |
|-------|---------|
| Summary | For offences regarding personal data, **TCC Arts. 135-140** apply. For breach of the erasure obligation in Art. 7, TCC Art. 138 applies |
| Operational Impact | Individual criminal liability; awareness for employees; assessment of report to the Public Prosecutor following internal investigation |
| Wiki | [11-denetim-ve-uyum/yaptirimlar-cezalar.md](../11-denetim-ve-uyum/yaptirimlar-cezalar.md) |

### ARTICLE 18 — Misdemeanours (Administrative Fines)
