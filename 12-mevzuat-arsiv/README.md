## Note on Legal Hierarchy

The Turkish hierarchy of legal sources:
1. Constitution (Art. 20 — privacy of private life, protection of personal data)
2. International conventions (Council of Europe Conventions 108 and 108+)
3. Law (KVKK No. 6698)
4. Presidential Decrees (where applicable)
5. Regulation (VERBİS Reg., Erasure Reg.)
6. Communiqué (Disclosure Communiqué, Application Communiqué)
7. Authority Decisions (administrative acts; binding)
8. Guides (interpretive sources; reflect Authority expectation)

- **Tertiary (interpretive):** Legal counsel opinions, academic articles, sector practice notes

> Wiki summaries are not a substitute for the source legislation. In a legal dispute or where interpretation is required, **the original legislative text** and the legal counsel's opinion are decisive.
