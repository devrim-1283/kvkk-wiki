## English

# Regulatory Change Monitoring Procedure and Impact Analysis

## 1. Purpose

To systematically monitor changes to KVKK and related legislation; to perform impact analysis quickly; to update policies, procedures, systems and training before compliance gaps emerge.

## 2. Monitoring Sources

### 2.1 Primary Sources (Authoritative)

| Source | URL / Access | Method | Frequency |
|--------|--------------|--------|-----------|
| Official Gazette | resmigazete.gov.tr | RSS + email subscription (filter on "KVKK") | Daily |
| KVKK Authority | kvkk.gov.tr | RSS + bulletin subscription; Decision Summaries page | Weekly |
| Legislation Information System | mevzuat.gov.tr | Version comparison | Monthly |
| Parliament (TBMM) | tbmm.gov.tr / Committee Reports | Watch list: Justice, Digital Media Committees | Monthly |
| Presidency | cbiko.gov.tr | Presidential Decrees | Monthly |

### 2.2 Secondary Sources (Informational)

| Source | Method | Frequency |
|--------|--------|-----------|
| Law firm bulletins (large firms) | Email subscription, RSS | Weekly |
| Sector associations (TÜSİAD, TBB, TÜBİSAD, TBV) | Bulletin + member portal | Monthly |
| Academic journals (KVKK, IT Law) | TR-Index, Google Scholar alerts | Quarterly |
| EDPB — GDPR commentary | edpb.europa.eu | Monthly (international alignment) |

### 2.3 Tertiary Sources (Important)

| Source | Method |
|--------|--------|
| Sector webinars, conferences | Annual schedule |
| Speeches, statements by Authority Chair / members | Social media, press search |
| Decision archive providers (Lexpera, Yetkin Hukuk etc.) | Premium subscription |

## 3. Monitoring Process

### 3.1 Monthly Scan

| Step | Owner | Deadline |
|------|-------|----------|
| Compile new legislation / Authority decision / decree from sources | Legal Counsel | 3rd business day of the month |
| Pre-classify relevance (no impact / watch / impact analysis) | Legal + KVKK Officer | 5th business day |
| Detailed analysis for those flagged for impact | Relevant owner | 10 business days |
| Add to KVKK Committee agenda | KVKK Officer | Monthly committee meeting |

### 3.2 Immediate Critical Changes

Immediate action without waiting for the monthly cycle for:
- KVKK amendment (parliamentary)
- Presidential Decree relating to KVKK
- Regulation amendment
- Communiqué amendment
- Principle decision (Authority precedent-setting decision)
- Revaluation rate communiqué (annual fine update — December)

Immediate response timing:
- Legal + KVKK Officer pre-analysis: 24 hours
- First impact scan: 5 business days
- Extraordinary KVKK Committee meeting: within 7 business days
- Action plan: within 14 business days

## 5. Version Control Procedure

### 5.1 Wiki Document Version Management

Each document carries metadata at the top:

```
Versiyon / Version: 1.0
Yürürlük / Effective: 2026-05-08
Gözden Geçirme / Review: Yıllık + tetiklenmiş — Annual + triggered
İlgili Mevzuat / Legal Reference: ...
```

### 5.2 Version Number Rules

| Version Type | When | Example |
|--------------|------|---------|
| Major (X.0) | Substantive change (legislative amendment, scope expansion) | 1.0 → 2.0 |
| Minor (X.Y) | Content update (additional article, procedure step) | 1.0 → 1.1 |
| Patch (X.Y.Z) | Correction (typo, link update) | 1.1 → 1.1.1 |

### 5.3 Version History

Each document file maintains a version history at the bottom or in a separate CHANGELOG:

```
## Version History

| Version | Date | Change | Author | Approver |
|---------|------|--------|--------|----------|
| 2.0 | 2026-04-01 | KVKK Art. 9 new regime alignment (Law 7499) | Legal | KVKK Committee |
| 1.1 | 2025-09-15 | Standard contract section added | KVKK Officer | KVKK Committee |
| 1.0 | 2025-01-15 | Initial publication | KVKK Officer | KVKK Committee |
```

### 5.4 Publication Process

| Step | Owner |
|------|-------|
| Draft preparation | Owner |
| Legal review | Legal Counsel |
| KVKK Officer approval | KVKK Officer |
| Committee approval (critical documents) | KVKK Committee |
| Wiki publication | KVKK Officer |
| Communication to stakeholders | KVKK Officer |
| Old version archived | KVKK Officer |

## 6. Quick Impact Classification Table

| Change Type | Typical Impact | First Action Time |
|-------------|----------------|--------------------|
| KVKK article amendment | Critical | 24 hours |
| Regulation amendment | High-Critical | 48 hours |
| Communiqué amendment | Medium-High | 5 business days |
| Authority principle decision | High | 5 business days |
| Complaint-precedent decision | Medium | 10 business days |
| Guide release / update | Medium | 10 business days |
| Revaluation rate | Low | December-January |
| Sector guide | Low-Medium | 30 days |

## 7. Annual Full Revision

Each year in **November**, all wiki documents and the legislation archive are fully revised:

- Are all referenced laws still in force?
- Are all Authority decisions captured?
- Are all version numbers current?
- Are cross-references unbroken?
- Are there topics to add (e.g. AI, cloud, IoT)?

Output: annual revision report, presentation to KVKK Committee, briefing to the Audit Committee of the Board.

## 9. Training and Communication

When a regulatory change has impact:
- KVKK Officer prepares a 1-page briefing for affected units.
- Integrated into training modules.
- Included in monthly KVKK bulletin.
- Coffee meeting / webinar for critical changes.

# Mevzuat Değişikliği Takip Prosedürü ve Etki Analizi

Şirketin tabi olduğu KVKK ve ilgili mevzuattaki değişiklikleri sistematik biçimde izlemek; etki analizini hızlı şekilde yapmak; uyum eksikliği ortaya çıkmadan politika, prosedür, sistem ve eğitimleri güncellemektir.

| KVKK Kurumu | kvkk.gov.tr | RSS + bültene abonelik; Karar Özetleri sayfası | Haftalık |
| Mevzuat Bilgi Sistemi | mevzuat.gov.tr | Versiyon karşılaştırma | Aylık |

| Adım | Sahip | Termin |
|------|-------|--------|

| Etki analizi gerekli olanlar için detaylı çalışma | İlgili sahip | 10 iş günü |
| KVKK Komitesi gündeme alınması | KVKK Sor. | Aylık komite toplantısı |

### 3.2 Anlık Kritik Değişiklikler

Aşağıdaki durumlar için aylık takvimi beklemeden anında aksiyon:
- 6698 sayılı Kanun değişikliği (yasama)
- KVKK ile ilgili Cumhurbaşkanlığı Kararnamesi
- Yönetmelik değişikliği
- Tebliğ değişikliği
- Genel ilke kararı (Kurul'un emsâl niteliğindeki kararı)
- Yeniden değerleme oranı tebliği (idari para cezası miktar güncellemesi — Aralık)

Anlık aksiyon süreleri:
- Hukuk + KVKK Sor. ön analiz: 24 saat
- İlk etki taraması: 5 iş günü
- KVKK Komitesi olağanüstü toplantısı: 7 iş günü içinde
- Aksiyon planı: 14 iş günü içinde
