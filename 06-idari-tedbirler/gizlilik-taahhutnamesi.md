## English

# Confidentiality and Personal Data Protection Undertakings

## 1. Purpose

To document, in a **legally binding** manner, the obligation of employees, consultants, interns, temporary staff, and vendor personnel to protect personal data and confidential information they gain access to during their tasks; to constitute the basis of disciplinary and legal process in case of breach. The operational implementation of the measure required under "Confidentiality Undertakings" in the KVKK Personal Data Security Guide.

## 2. Principles

1. **Inclusiveness:** All physical/digital assets accessing personal data have a signed undertaking.
2. **Clarity:** The employee must clearly understand what type of information they are protecting, what type of obligation they have, and what the consequences are in case of breach.
3. **Continuity:** The obligation continues during employment **and afterwards**.
4. **Documentation:** Wet or electronic signature, kept in personnel file and separate audit archive.
5. **Linkage with Training:** Undertaking signature day is contemporaneous with orientation training; an undertaking signed without training is ineffective.
6. **Compliance with Legislation:** Full reference to Turkish Criminal Code Art. 135-140 and KVKK regulations.
7. **Balanced:** Proportional between employer interest and employee rights; cannot be abused.

## 3. Undertaking Types

| Type | For Whom | Signing Time | Retention |
|-----|----------|-------------|---------|
| Employee KVKK and Confidentiality Undertaking | All employees | Orientation (first day) | Personnel file + HR archive (employment + 10 years) |
| Manager / Senior Additional Undertaking | Manager+ level | At assignment date | Personnel file |
| Special-Category Data Access Undertaking | HR, health, legal, etc. with access | Before access granted | Personnel + KVKK Officer archive |
| Intern Undertaking | Intern | First day | Intern file |
| Consultant / External Personnel Undertaking | Contracted consultants, freelancers | At contract signing | Legal archive |
| Vendor Personnel Undertaking | Persons provided by vendor | With access request form | Contract file |
| Temporary / Project Personnel Undertaking | Persons with defined project duration | At assignment date | Project file + HR |

## 4. Employee KVKK and Confidentiality Undertaking — Full Template

> **Important:** The following text is a **template** and must not be used without final approval by the company lawyer. For sectoral additional regulations (BDDK, health, defense), additional clauses are added.

```
PERSONAL DATA PROTECTION AND CONFIDENTIALITY UNDERTAKING

Parties
Employer: <Full Company Title> ("Company")
Address: <Address>
MERSIS / VERBIS No: <Number>

Employee:
Name and Surname: ............................................
Turkish ID No: ...............................................
Personnel No: ................................................
Position: ....................................................
Department: ..................................................
Date of Hire: ................................................

1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE

This Undertaking governs the obligations of the Employee, within the scope
of Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data (KVKK), Articles
135-140 of the Turkish Criminal Code, Article 396 of the Turkish Code of
Obligations and other relevant legislation, regarding personal data and
confidential information that the Employee may access during their tasks
performed within the Company.

2. DEFINITIONS

2.1. Personal Data: Any information relating to an identified or identifiable
natural person (KVKK Art. 3).

2.2. Special-Category Personal Data: Within the scope of KVKK Art. 6,
data concerning race, ethnic origin, political opinion, philosophical belief,
religion, sect, dress, association, foundation or trade union membership,
health, sexual life, criminal convictions and security measures, as well as
biometric and genetic data.

2.3. Confidential Information: All commercial, financial, technical, legal
and administrative information of the Company; production secrets, customer
lists, product development information, pricing, financial statements,
strategic plans, source code, architectural drawings, contracts, patent
applications, employee information and the like.

2.4. Processing: As defined in KVKK Art. 3, any operation performed on
personal data such as obtaining, recording, storing, preserving, modifying,
re-arranging, disclosing, transferring, taking over, making available,
classifying, or preventing the use of.

3. GENERAL OBLIGATIONS

3.1. The Employee undertakes to process personal data and confidential
information that they may access or become aware of due to their duties
only for the purposes determined by the Company and within the scope of
their job description and authority.

3.2. The Employee undertakes that, for any reason, outside the scope of
their duty, they will not, with respect to personal data:
   a) Collect,
   b) View,
   c) Copy,
   d) Reproduce,
   e) Take outside the Company,
   f) Share with third parties,
   g) Transfer to personal device / account,
   h) Share on social media or in publicly accessible spaces.

3.3. The Employee undertakes not to share with anyone, or allow others to
use, the username, password, and other credentials provided to them for
access to personal data.

3.4. The Employee undertakes not to access personal data using another
person's account/identity.

4. TECHNICAL OBLIGATIONS

The Employee undertakes to comply with all policies and procedures in
force, including the Company's Information Security Policy, Access
Management Policy, Acceptable Use Policy, Mobile and Remote Work Policy,
Clean Desk - Clean Screen Policy, and KVKK Policy; and to refrain from
acts violating these policies. These policies have been presented to the
Employee during the orientation process and are available via the intranet.

5. ADDITIONAL OBLIGATION REGARDING SPECIAL-CATEGORY PERSONAL DATA

If the Employee accesses special-category personal data (especially health,
biometric) during the duties, the Employee undertakes to fully comply with
the additional security measures and access boundaries defined by the
Company for such data; to process such data only for explicit and limited
purposes, and under no circumstances to record and take it outside.

6. TRANSFER AND THIRD-PARTY SHARING

The Employee knows that any internal or external transfer of personal data
can be made only within the scope of Company policies, with necessary
approvals, and within the scope of KVKK Art. 8 and Art. 9; and undertakes
to refrain from unauthorized transfers.

7. SOCIAL ENGINEERING AND NOTIFICATION OBLIGATION

The Employee undertakes to immediately report to the Company's Information
Security Team / KVKK Officer any social engineering attempts (telephone,
email, personal contact, etc.) aimed at obtaining personal data and
confidential information.

8. DATA BREACH NOTIFICATION

The Employee undertakes to report any situation suspected to be a personal
data breach (lost / stolen device or paper document, wrong email send,
unauthorized access suspicion, open sharing, etc.) without delay and at
most within 24 hours through the Company's breach notification channel
(kvkk@<company>.com.tr / +90 ... / extension ...).

A report made in good faith and without delay cannot be the basis for any
disciplinary action against the Employee; on the contrary, delaying or
hiding a report constitutes a breach in itself.

9. POST-EMPLOYMENT OBLIGATION

The Employee undertakes that, indefinitely from the termination of the
employment contract;
   a) Will not use the Company's personal data and confidential information
      in any way,
   b) Will not share with third parties,
   c) Will not turn into personal advantage (including new workplace),
   d) Will return to the Company all information and data that may have
      been previously copied / kept, without leaving a copy, and will
      permanently delete from personal devices.

10. CRIMINAL AND LEGAL LIABILITY

10.1. The Employee accepts to be aware of the provisions of KVKK Art. 17,
Turkish Criminal Code Art. 135 (Recording of Personal Data),
Art. 136 (Unlawful Disclosure or Acquisition of Data), Art. 137 (Qualified
Cases), Art. 138 (Failure to Destroy Data).

10.2. The Employee accepts and undertakes that, in case of breach of the
obligations in this Undertaking:
   a) The employment contract may be terminated for just cause under
      Labor Law Art. 25/II,
   b) Compensation for any damage that the Company may suffer,
   c) Liability under criminal law for acts that may be subject to
      criminal prosecution,
   d) Sanctions to be applied under the Discipline Regulation,
   e) That all payments (including data subject compensation,
      administrative fine, court costs, attorney fees) the Company may
      have to make in lawsuits filed by third parties may be recoursed
      to the Employee.

11. PRIVACY NOTICE

Within the scope of KVKK Art. 10, the Employee declares to have been
informed by the Company's Employee Privacy Notice regarding the purposes
of processing of personal data belonging to them by the Company, the
legal grounds, the parties to which they may be transferred, and the
data subject rights.

12. AUDIT AND MONITORING

The Employee declares that they know and accept that, as stated in the
Company's IT Use Policy and Employee Privacy Notice, audit and monitoring
may be carried out, within legal limits and for security purposes, on the
use of corporate email, file sharing, web proxy, endpoint software and
applications.

13. UNLIMITED DURATION AND SEVERABILITY

The confidentiality and personal data protection obligations of this
Undertaking are unlimited in duration; if any provision of the Undertaking
is deemed invalid or unenforceable, the validity of other provisions is
not affected.

14. APPLICABLE LAW AND COMPETENT COURT

This Undertaking is subject to the laws of the Republic of Turkey. In case
of dispute, the Courts and Enforcement Offices of Istanbul (<city>) are
competent.

15. SUPPORTING DOCUMENTS

The Employee declares to have read, understood and received a copy of the
following documents:
   - KVKK Policy
   - Information Security Policy
   - Employee Privacy Notice
   - Acceptable Use Policy

Signatures

Employee
Date: ____ / ____ / ________
Signature:

Company Representative (HR)
Date: ____ / ____ / ________
Signature:
```

## 5. Manager / Senior Additional Undertaking

Additional undertaking content to be signed by senior and managerial employees in addition to the main undertaking:

```
ADDITIONAL UNDERTAKING (MANAGER)

A. RESPONSIBILITY FOR TEAM MEMBERS

The Manager is primarily responsible for the team's compliance with this
KVKK and Confidentiality Undertaking. The Manager is in particular
obligated to ensure that:
   a) Orientation training and signing of undertaking of new team members
      are completed on time,
   b) The process of updating access rights is initiated for position
      changes (Mover),
   c) All access of leaving team members (Leaver) is closed on the last
      working day,
   d) The team's annual training refreshers are completed on time,
   e) Reports of any security violation suspicions observed in the team.

B. STRATEGIC INFORMATION PROTECTION

The Manager is additionally obligated to protect strategic plan, customer
portfolio, product roadmap, financial projection, M&A information, and
sensitive HR decisions accessed during their duties.

C. MEDIA AND PUBLIC OPINION

The Manager accepts that they may comment / share to media / public /
social media on issues such as personal data breach, investigation, court
process and similar only when directed by the Communications Directorate
and the Legal Counsel.

D. NON-COMPETE / NON-SOLICITATION OF EMPLOYEES

The non-compete and non-solicitation provisions separately governed in the
employment contract continue to be in force independently of this
Undertaking.

Signature:
```

## 6. Special-Category Data Access Additional Undertaking

Additional text for those with regular access to special-category data, such as HR, occupational physician, health personnel, legal, internal audit:

```
SPECIAL-CATEGORY PERSONAL DATA ACCESS ADDITIONAL UNDERTAKING

The Employee knows that they will access special-category personal data
within the scope of KVKK Art. 6 due to their duties and accepts the
following additional obligations:

1. Access shall be used only as necessary for the defined task; access
   for "curiosity" purposes constitutes a violation.

2. Records containing special-category data:
   a) Cannot be printed, photocopied, screenshot, photographed (except
      for limited authorized exceptions).
   b) Cannot be shared via email or messaging.
   c) Cannot be stored outside encrypted, auditable systems provided by
      the Company.

3. The Employee knows that every special-category data access event
   is recorded in the audit log and is subject to a justified access
   policy.

4. If health data is involved, the Employee is subject to the obligation
   of medical secrecy (Medical Deontology Regulation, professional ethics
   rules).

Signature:
```

## 10. Signing Process

### 10.1. New Starting Employee

```
Day 1 (Orientation)
   08:30 - 09:00   Welcome, document submission
   09:00 - 10:30   KVKK + Information Security basic presentation
   10:30 - 11:00   Policy documents review
   11:00 - 11:30   Q&A
   11:30 - 12:00   Undertaking signatures (main + supplementary if applicable)
   ...
```

Signing not all at once, but **conscious signing after training**. HR personnel oversee the signing.

### 10.2. Retention

- Wet signed: in personnel file, locked archive, with digital scan.
- Electronic signature: e-signature infrastructure compliant with KVKK Regulation (Law No. 5070 + Financial Stamp/e-signature); meta data (timestamp, IP) kept.
- Retention period: throughout employment + 10 years after departure (statute of limitations).

### 10.3. Version Change

- When the undertaking is updated, all active employees sign the new version (within 90 days).
- Old version remains valid until breach moment; the new version takes effect.

## 11. Process in Case of Breach

### 11.1. Detection

- Detection automatic (DLP, SIEM, IR) or manual (manager, notification line, audit).

### 11.2. Disciplinary Process

KVKK / confidentiality violation classification:

| Class | Example | Sanction |
|-------|-------|----------|
| **Light** | Email to wrong recipient (short duration, recipient returned, limited data) | Written warning + additional training |
| **Medium** | Password sharing, unauthorized data copy to portable device | Written warning + disciplinary board + cut |
| **Severe** | Bulk data copy, external sharing, intentional disclosure | Termination + compensation lawsuit + report to prosecutor's office (TCC Art. 135-138) |
| **Critical** | Data trafficking, organized breach, privilege escalation | Termination + criminal lawsuit + chain liability |

### 11.3. Process Rules

- The Employee is granted the right of defense (written + oral).
- Disciplinary board (HR + Legal + relevant Director + KVKK Officer representative).
- Decision communicated in writing.
- Right of appeal (10 business days).
- Independent legal process (administrative + criminal) is run in parallel with the HR process.
- Whole process reported to the KVKK Committee.

### 11.4. Evidence

- DLP/EDR/SIEM records + chain of custody.
- The employee's written defense.
- Witness statements (if needed).
- Forensic disk image (in severe breach).

## 12. Additional Undertaking for KVKK Officer / IR Team

KVKK Officer and Incident Response Team members sign an additional undertaking, recording the obligation to protect **employee-specific data accessed during investigation** (confidentiality of confidentiality). This text is included as additional under the main undertaking under "personal data learned during investigation".

## 13. Retention, Access and Privacy

- The undertaking is in **locked** environment in the HR archive.
- Digital copy encrypted, with restricted access (HR + Legal + KVKK Officer).
- The Employee can request a copy of their own undertaking at any time.
- In requests like KVKK Authority audit, masked (personal data minimized) presentation.

- [ ] Are manager, special-category, intern, consultant, vendor variants present?
- [ ] Is the new starter signing within orientation week (100%)?

- [ ] Are vendor contracts placing this form as additional for vendor personnel?
- [ ] Does the Employee receive a copy?

- [ ] Are evidence + defense + appeal steps of the disciplinary process documented?

## 15. Annual Reminder

During the annual refresh training, the employee is given the right to read their undertaking; policy changes are summarized. Even if intentional re-signing is not done, **reminder**, is reported annually to the KVKK Committee.

## 16. Common Mistakes

- Signing taken without training — no informed consent, weak legal effect.
- Signing the contract only with the vendor company without reaching the vendor personnel.
- Skipping intern parental approval.
- Not taking "return record" at departure.
- Re-signing not done by all active employees on version change.
- Right of defense violated in disciplinary process — leads to invalidation of termination.
- Missing arbitration / jurisdiction / law selection clause.
- Electronic signature not compliant with Law No. 5070 (simple "click" insufficient).

## Türkçe

# Gizlilik ve Kişisel Veri Koruma Taahhütnameleri

## 1. Amaç

Çalışanların, danışmanların, stajyerlerin, geçici personelin ve tedarikçi personelinin görevleri sırasında erişim kazandığı kişisel veri ve gizli bilgiyi koruma yükümlülüğünü **hukuki olarak bağlayıcı** biçimde belgelemek; ihlal halinde disiplin ve hukuki sürecin temelini oluşturmak. KVKK Veri Güvenliği Rehberi'nde "Gizlilik Taahhütnameleri" başlığı altında talep edilen tedbirin operasyonel uygulamasıdır.

## 2. İlkeler

1. **Kapsayıcılık:** Kişisel veriye erişen tüm fiziksel/dijital varlıklar imzalı taahhütnameye sahiptir.
2. **Açıklık:** Çalışan ne tür bilgiyi koruduğunu, ne tür yükümlülüğü olduğunu, ihlal halinde sonucu açık biçimde anlamalıdır.
3. **Devamlılık:** Yükümlülük istihdam süresince **ve sonrasında** devam eder.
4. **Belgeleme:** Islak veya elektronik imza, özlük dosyasında ve ayrı denetim arşivinde saklanır.
5. **Eğitim ile Bağlantı:** Taahhütname imza günü oryantasyon eğitimiyle aynı zamanda yapılır; eğitim olmadan imzalanan taahhüt etkisiz kalır.
6. **Mevzuata Uyum:** TCK m.135-140 ve KVKK düzenlemelerine tam atıf.
7. **Dengeli:** İşveren menfaati ile çalışan hakları arasında orantılı; kötüye kullanılamaz.

## 3. Taahhütname Türleri

| Tür | Kim İçin | İmza Zamanı | Saklama |
|-----|----------|-------------|---------|
| Çalışan KVKK ve Gizlilik Taahhütnamesi | Tüm çalışanlar | Oryantasyon (ilk gün) | Özlük dosyası + İK arşiv (istihdam + 10 yıl) |
| Yönetici / Üst Düzey Ek Taahhütnamesi | Müdür+ kademe | Atama tarihinde | Özlük dosyası |
| Özel Nitelikli Veri Erişim Taahhütnamesi | İK, sağlık, hukuk vb. erişim alacaklar | Erişim verilmeden önce | Özlük + KVKK Sorumlusu arşivi |
| Stajyer Taahhütnamesi | Stajyer | İlk gün | Stajyer dosyası |
| Danışman / Dış Personel Taahhütnamesi | Sözleşmeli danışman, freelancer | Sözleşme imzası ile | Hukuk arşivi |
| Tedarikçi Personeli Taahhütnamesi | Tedarikçi tarafından sağlanan kişiler | Erişim talep formuyla | Sözleşme dosyası |
| Geçici / Proje Personeli Taahhütnamesi | Proje süresi belli kişiler | Atama tarihinde | Proje dosyası + İK |

```

TC Kimlik No: .........................................

Pozisyon: .............................................
Departman: ............................................
İşe Başlama Tarihi: ...................................

1. AMAÇ VE KAPSAM

İşbu Taahhütname; 6698 sayılı Kişisel Verilerin Korunması Kanunu (KVKK), Türk
Ceza Kanunu m.135-140, Türk Borçlar Kanunu m.396 ve diğer ilgili mevzuat
kapsamında, Çalışanın Şirket bünyesinde yürüttüğü görevler sırasında
erişebileceği kişisel veri ve gizli bilgilere ilişkin yükümlülüklerini
düzenlemektedir.

2. TANIMLAR

2.1. Kişisel Veri: Kimliği belirli veya belirlenebilir gerçek kişiye ilişkin
her türlü bilgi (KVKK m.3).

2.2. Özel Nitelikli Kişisel Veri: KVKK m.6 kapsamında ırk, etnik köken, siyasi
düşünce, felsefi inanç, din, mezhep, kılık ve kıyafet, dernek, vakıf ya da
sendika üyeliği, sağlık, cinsel hayat, ceza mahkumiyeti ve güvenlik
tedbirleriyle ilgili veriler ile biyometrik ve genetik veriler.

2.3. Gizli Bilgi: Şirketin ticari, mali, teknik, hukuki, idari her türlü
bilgisi; üretim sırları, müşteri listeleri, ürün geliştirme bilgileri,
fiyatlandırma, finansal tablolar, stratejik planlar, kaynak kodu, mimari
çizimler, sözleşmeler, patent başvuruları, çalışan bilgileri ve benzerleri.

2.4. İşleme: KVKK m.3'te tanımlandığı şekilde, kişisel verilerin elde
edilmesi, kaydedilmesi, depolanması, muhafaza edilmesi, değiştirilmesi,
yeniden düzenlenmesi, açıklanması, aktarılması, devralınması, elde
edilebilir hale getirilmesi, sınıflandırılması ya da kullanılmasının
engellenmesi gibi her türlü işlem.

3. GENEL YÜKÜMLÜLÜKLER

3.1. Çalışan, görevi gereği erişeceği veya farkına varacağı kişisel veri ve
gizli bilgiyi yalnızca Şirket'in belirlediği amaçlar doğrultusunda, görev
tanımı ve yetki kapsamında işleyeceğini taahhüt eder.

3.2. Çalışan, hangi gerekçeyle olursa olsun, görev kapsamı dışında kişisel
veriyi:
   a) Toplamayacağını,
   b) Görüntülemeyeceğini,
   c) Kopyalamayacağını,
   d) Çoğaltmayacağını,
   e) Şirket dışına çıkarmayacağını,
   f) Üçüncü taraflarla paylaşmayacağını,
   g) Şahsi cihazına / hesabına aktarmayacağını,
   h) Sosyal medyada veya kamuya açık alanda paylaşmayacağını
taahhüt eder.

3.3. Çalışan, kişisel verilere erişim için kendisine sağlanan kullanıcı adı,
şifre ve diğer kimlik bilgilerini başkasıyla paylaşmayacağını, başkasının
kullanımına izin vermeyeceğini taahhüt eder.

3.4. Çalışan, başkasının hesabını/kimliğini kullanarak kişisel veriye
```

## 10. İmza Süreci

### 10.1. Yeni Başlayan Çalışan

```
Gün 1 (Oryantasyon)
   08:30 - 09:00   Karşılama, evrak teslim
   09:00 - 10:30   KVKK + Bilgi Güvenliği temel sunum
   10:30 - 11:00   Politika dokümanları gözden geçirme
   11:00 - 11:30   Soru-cevap
   11:30 - 12:00   Taahhütname imzaları (ana + uygunsa ek)
   ...
```

İmza tek seferde değil, **eğitim sonrası bilinçli imza**. İK personeli imzaya nezaret eder.

### 10.2. Saklama

- Islak imzalı: özlük dosyasında, kilitli arşiv, dijital tarama da yapılır.
- Elektronik imza: KVKK Yönetmeliği'ne uygun e-imza altyapısı (5070 sayılı Kanun + Mali Mühür/e-imza); meta veri (zaman damgası, IP) saklanır.
- Saklama süresi: istihdam süresince + 10 yıl ayrılış sonrası (zamanaşımı).

### 10.3. Versiyon Değişikliği

- Taahhütname güncellendiğinde tüm aktif çalışanlar yeni sürümü imzalar (90 gün içinde).
- Eski sürüm geçerli olarak korunur ihlal anına kadar; yeni sürüm yürürlüğe girer.

## 11. İhlal Halinde Süreç

### 11.1. Tespit

- Otomatik (DLP, SIEM, IR) veya manuel (yönetici, bildirim hattı, denetim) tespit.

### 11.2. Disiplin Süreci

KVKK / gizlilik ihlal sınıflandırması:

| Sınıf | Örnek | Yaptırım |
|-------|-------|----------|
| **Hafif** | Yanlış aliciya e-posta (kısa süreli, alıcı geri gönderdi, sınırlı veri) | Yazılı uyarı + ek eğitim |
| **Orta** | Şifre paylaşımı, taşınabilir cihaza yetkisiz veri kopyalama | Yazılı uyarı + disiplin kurulu + kesinti |
| **Ağır** | Toplu veri kopyalama, dış paylaşım, kasıtlı ifşa | Fesih + tazminat davası + savcılığa suç duyurusu (TCK m.135-138) |
| **Kritik** | Veri ticareti, organize ihlal, yetki yükseltme | Fesih + ceza davası + zincirleme sorumluluk |

### 11.3. Süreç Kuralları

- Çalışana savunma hakkı verilir (yazılı + sözlü).
- Disiplin kurulu (İK + Hukuk + ilgili Direktör + KVKK Sorumlusu temsilcisi).
- Karar yazılı tebliğ.
- İtiraz hakkı (10 iş günü).
- Bağımsız hukuki süreç (idari + ceza) İK süreciyle paralel yürütülür.
- Tüm süreç KVKK Komitesi'ne raporlanır.

### 11.4. Kanıt

- DLP/EDR/SIEM kayıtları + chain of custody.
- Çalışanın yazılı savunması.
- Tanık ifadeleri (gerekirse).
- Forensic disk imajı (ağır ihlalde).

## 12. KVKK Sorumlusu / IR Ekibi Ek Taahhüt

KVKK Sorumlusu ve İhlal Müdahale Ekibi üyeleri ek bir taahhütnameyle, soruşturma sürecinde **ulaştıkları çalışan-spesifik verileri** koruma yükümlülüğünü imzalar (gizliliğin gizliliği). Bu metin, ana taahhütnamenin "soruşturma esnasında öğrenilen kişisel veriler" başlığı altında ek olarak yer alır.

## 13. Saklama, Erişim ve Mahremiyet

- Taahhütname İK arşivinde **kilitli** ortamda.
- Dijital kopya şifreli, erişim sınırlı (İK + Hukuk + KVKK Sorumlusu).
- Çalışan kendi taahhütname kopyasını her zaman talep edebilir.
- KVKK Kurulu denetimi gibi taleplerde maskeli (kişisel veri minimize) sunum.

## 14. Kontrol Listesi

- [ ] Ana taahhütname şablonu Hukuk + KVKK Sorumlusu onaylı, ≤12 ay güncel mi?
- [ ] Yönetici, özel nitelikli, stajyer, danışman, tedarikçi varyantları mevcut mu?
- [ ] Yeni başlayan oryantasyon haftası içinde imzalıyor (%100)?
- [ ] İmza eğitim sonrası, bilinçli rıza ile alınıyor mu?
- [ ] Islak/elektronik imza saklama süresi (istihdam + 10 yıl) belgeli mi?
- [ ] Tedarikçi sözleşmelerinde tedarikçi personeli için bu form ek olarak konuyor mu?
- [ ] Çalışan kopyasını alıyor mu?
- [ ] Versiyon değişiminde yeniden imza süreci yürüyor mu?
- [ ] Disiplin sürecinin kanıt + savunma + itiraz adımları yazılı mı?
- [ ] TCK m.135-140 atıflı, KVKK m.12 atıflı maddeler güncel mi?
- [ ] Aydınlatma Metni eki taahhütnameyle birlikte sunuluyor mu?
- [ ] Ayrılış sırasında "iade ve süresiz yükümlülük" hatırlatması imzalı mı?

- [ ] Soruşturma kanıt zinciri prosedürü hazır mı?

- [ ] İmza dosyalarına erişim kontrol listesi yıllık review'da mı?

## 15. Yıllık Hatırlatma

Yıllık tazeleme eğitimi sırasında çalışana taahhütnamesi okuma hakkı sağlanır; politika değişiklikleri özetlenir. Kasıtlı imza-yenilenme yapılmasa bile **hatırlatma**, KVKK Komitesi'ne yıllık raporlanır.

## 16. Yaygın Hatalar

- Eğitim olmadan imza alınması — bilinçli rıza yok, hukuki etkisi zayıf.
- Tedarikçi personeline ulaşılmadan sadece tedarikçi şirketle sözleşme yapılması.
- Stajyer veli onayı atlanması.
- Ayrılış sırasında "iade tutanağı" alınmaması.
- Versiyon değişiminde tüm aktif çalışanlara yeniden imza yapılmaması.
- Disiplin sürecinde savunma hakkı ihlali — fesih iptaline yol açar.
- Tahkimname / yetki / kanun seçimi maddesi eksikliği.
- Elektronik imzanın 5070 sayılı Kanun'a uygun olmaması (basit "tikleme" yetersiz).
