## English

# Personal Data Retention and Destruction Policy — Full Template

> This document contains all minimum elements (Reg. Art. 6) of the Personal Data Retention and Destruction Policy that is **mandatory** under Reg. Art. 5(1) for **data controllers subject to the VERBİS registration obligation**, in completed form. The document is published after being adapted to the corporate identity; structural headings and content elements must be preserved.

> **Important note (Reg. Art. 5(2)):** The mere existence of a policy does not mean that personal data is retained/destroyed in compliance with the Law and the Regulation. A policy is a compliance instrument only when it is actually applied, audited and updated.

---

## 1. Purpose of Preparation (Reg. Art. 6/1-a)

This Policy has been prepared to ensure that personal data processing activities carried out by [COMPANY NAME A.Ş.] ("Company") comply with the principle in Article 4(2)(d) of Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data ("Law") that personal data shall be retained "only for the period stipulated in the relevant legislation or required for the purpose of processing," together with Articles 7 and 12 of the same Law and the provisions of the Regulation on Erasure, Destruction or Anonymization of Personal Data ("Regulation").

The specific purposes of the Policy are:
- To determine the procedures and principles for retention and destruction of personal data,
- To clarify the responsibilities of units involved in retention and destruction processes,
- To set out the framework and schedule of periodic destruction,
- To regulate the management of data subject requests,
- To determine the content and retention period of records that must be kept under Reg. Art. 7(3).

## 2. Scope (Reg. Art. 6/1-b)

### 2.1. Scope of Persons

This Policy covers all natural persons whose personal data is processed by the Company, including employees, candidates, former employees, customers, prospects, visitors, supplier officials, shareholders, partners and contracted third parties.

### 2.2. Scope of Data

All personal data and special categories of personal data processed by fully or partially automated means or by non-automated means as part of any data filing system are within the scope of this Policy.

### 2.3. Recording Media (Reg. Art. 6/1-b)

The Policy applies to personal data residing in the following recording media:

| Medium Type | Examples |
|-------------|----------|
| Electronic — server | Database servers, file servers, mail servers, AD/LDAP, ERP, CRM, HR applications |
| Electronic — endpoint | Laptops/desktops, mobile devices, removable media (USB, external disk) |
| Electronic — cloud | IaaS/PaaS/SaaS providers, object storage, backup storage, log storage |
| Electronic — network | Firewall logs, IPS/IDS logs, proxy logs, DNS logs |
| Paper | Contract files, personnel files, application forms, visitor logs |
| Visual/audio | CCTV recordings, call center voice recordings, meeting recordings |
| Other | Magnetic cards, backup tapes, optical media |

### 2.4. Scope of Units

The Policy covers all units within the Company and all data processors (suppliers, service providers) contracted on behalf of the legal entity.

## 4. Reasons Requiring Retention and Destruction (Reg. Art. 6/1-ç)

### 4.1. Legal Reasons Requiring Retention

The Company retains personal data based on the following legal grounds:

- Law No. 6698 (KVKK)
- Turkish Code of Obligations No. 6098 (statute of limitation periods)
- Turkish Commercial Code No. 6102 (book and document retention)
- Tax Procedure Law No. 213 (retention of tax documents)
- Labor Law No. 4857 (personnel file)
- Social Insurance and General Health Insurance Law No. 5510
- Law No. 5651 (regulation of internet broadcasts)
- Consumer Protection Law No. 6502
- Banking Law No. 5411 (to the extent applicable)
- Payment and Securities Settlement Systems Law No. 6493 (to the extent applicable)
- Law No. 5549 on the Prevention of Laundering of Proceeds of Crime (MASAK)
- Financial Leasing, Factoring and Financing Companies Law No. 6361 (to the extent applicable)
- Relevant sectoral legislation (BDDK, SPK, EPDK, Ministry of Health, etc.)

### 4.2. Other Reasons Requiring Retention

- Performance of contracts and contractual obligations,
- Communications required by the legal relationship with the data subject,
- The Company's legitimate interest (audit, reporting, internal investigation),
- Establishment, exercise or protection of a right (likelihood of litigation, dispute periods),
- Statistical and analytical reporting (after anonymization),
- Existence of explicit consent.

### 4.3. Reasons Requiring Destruction

In the following cases, the Company **erases, destroys or anonymizes** personal data ex officio or upon the data subject's request:

- The processing purpose has ended and there is no legal basis to continue retaining,
- Explicit consent that was the basis of processing has been withdrawn and no other condition exists,
- All processing conditions in KVKK Art. 5/Art. 6 have ceased,
- The data subject has requested erasure or destruction under KVKK Art. 11/(e)-(f) and the request is found to be justified,
- Processing must cease for compliance with Art. 12,
- The maximum period set by legislation has expired.

## 5. Technical and Administrative Measures — Retention (Reg. Art. 6/1-d)

### 5.1. Technical Measures

| Measure | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Access control | Access to personal data on a "need-to-know" basis, role-based access control (RBAC). Privileged Access Management (PAM) for privileged accounts |
| Authorization matrix | An Authorization Matrix is maintained for each system and reviewed quarterly |
| Logging | Database accesses, file accesses and privileged operations are logged for at least 1 year |
| Encryption | Sensitive data at rest is encrypted with AES-256, in transit with TLS 1.2+ |
| Key management | KMS/HSM is used; key rotation is annual or triggered |
| Data masking | Production data is not used in test/development environments; if it must be, it is masked |
| Backup | Backups are encrypted; access to backup storage is restricted; backup destruction calendar is policy-aligned |
| Penetration testing | Performed annually; findings reported to the risk committee |
| Vulnerability management | Monthly scan, monthly patching; 72 hours for critical vulnerabilities |
| Firewall/IPS | Network segmentation, keeping systems containing personal data in DMZ or secure zone |
| Anti-malware | On all endpoints and servers; real-time protection + EDR |
| DLP | Data loss prevention at egress points (email, USB, web) |
| Logging and SIEM | Centralized log management, correlation rules, 24/7 monitoring |

### 5.2. Administrative Measures

| Measure | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Policies and procedures | This Policy, Breach Management Procedure, Access Management Procedure |
| Training and awareness | Annual mandatory training; orientation training at hire; additional training for critical units |
| Confidentiality undertakings | Obtained from all employees and supplier data processors |
| Personal data processing inventory | Kept continuously up to date; ownership rests with the KVKK Officer |
| Risk assessment | Annual and process-triggered DPIA/KVKK risk analysis |
| Vendor audit | Destruction commitments in data processor contracts; annual vendor audit |
| Conflict of interest | The KVKK Officer cannot serve in a position determining processing purpose/means |
| Breach management | 72-hour notification line, breach record system |

## 6. Technical and Administrative Measures — Destruction (Reg. Art. 6/1-e)

### 6.1. Technical Measures

| Measure | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Automated retention | Automatic delete jobs in databases by record type |
| Backup destruction | Secure destruction of old backups under backup rotation policy |
| Crypto-erase | Effectively rendering data inaccessible by destroying the key in cloud or shared storage |
| Hardware destruction | Disk degauss + physical shredding; medium destruction record |
| Log cleansing | Erasure/anonymization of personal data fragments in logs whose retention period expired |
| Chain of evidence | Hash, screenshots, records for all destruction operations; chain of custody |

### 6.2. Administrative Measures

| Measure | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Authorization | Two-person approval for destruction (KVKK Officer + relevant unit lead) |
| Training | Specialized training for those involved in destruction |
| Documentation | Destruction record, periodic destruction report, annual summary |
| Vendor contract | A clause "destruction within 30 days upon contract termination" in data processor contracts |
| Record retention | Destruction records are retained for at least 3 years (Reg. Art. 7(3)), excluding other legal obligations |

## 9. Period of Periodic Destruction (Reg. Art. 6/1-ğ and Art. 11/2)

The Company performs periodic destruction **every six (6) months**. The periodic destruction calendar is:

| Period | Date Range | Responsibility |
|--------|------------|----------------|
| 1st periodic destruction | May each year | KVKK Officer (coordination), IT Operations (execution) |
| 2nd periodic destruction | November each year | KVKK Officer (coordination), IT Operations (execution) |

Erasure/destruction requests under Law Art. 11/(e)-(f) are concluded **within 30 days** and notification is given for data transferred to third parties (Reg. Art. 12).

For the detailed process flow see: [periyodik-imha-prosedur.md](periyodik-imha-prosedur.md)

## 10. Destruction Methods

Method selection is made based on medium and data category. For details see: [imha-yontemleri.md](imha-yontemleri.md)

Pursuant to Reg. Art. 7(5), the data controller selects the destruction method **ex officio**; upon the data subject's request, it selects the appropriate method, explaining the reason. Method selection considers the structure of the medium, the nature of the data and the risk of re-accessibility.

## 11. Data Subject Requests

The data subject may request erasure or destruction under KVKK Art. 11. Pursuant to Reg. Art. 12, the Company:

- If all processing conditions for the requested data have ceased: The request is concluded **within 30 days**; the data subject is informed.
- If data has been transferred to third parties: The third party is notified; actions under the Regulation are taken at the third party.
- If processing conditions have not entirely ceased: The request is rejected with reasons under KVKK Art. 13/3; the rejection is communicated in writing or electronically within 30 days.

For the process flow see: [09-ilgili-kisi-basvurulari/](../09-ilgili-kisi-basvurulari/)

### 4.3. İmhayı Gerektiren Sebepler

Aşağıdaki hallerde Şirket, kişisel verileri resen veya ilgili kişinin talebi üzerine **siler, yok eder veya anonim hale getirir**:

- İşleme faaliyetinin amacı sona ermiş ve ilgili mevzuat veya başka bir hukuki dayanak gereği saklamaya gerek kalmamışsa,
- İşlemenin dayanağı olan açık rıza geri alınmışsa ve başka bir işleme şartı yoksa,
- KVKK m.5/m.6'daki tüm işleme şartları ortadan kalkmışsa,
- İlgili kişi, KVKK m.11/(e)-(f) uyarınca silme veya yok etme talebinde bulunmuş ve talep yerinde görülmüşse,
- Kanun m.12'ye uygunluk kapsamında işlemenin durması gerekiyorsa,
- Mevzuatın belirlediği maksimum süre dolmuşsa.

Detaylı süreç akışı için: [periyodik-imha-prosedur.md](periyodik-imha-prosedur.md)

## 10. İmha Yöntemleri

Yöntem seçimi, ortam ve veri kategorisine göre yapılır. Detay için: [imha-yontemleri.md](imha-yontemleri.md)

Yön. m.7(5) uyarınca veri sorumlusu, **resen** imha yöntemini kendisi seçer; ilgili kişi talebi halinde uygun yöntemi gerekçesini açıklayarak seçer. Yöntem seçiminde ortamın yapısı, verinin niteliği ve tekrar erişilebilirlik riski dikkate alınır.

## 11. İlgili Kişi Talepleri

İlgili kişi, KVKK m.11 uyarınca silme veya yok etme talep edebilir. Şirket, Yön. m.12 uyarınca:

- Talebe konu kişisel verilerin işleme şartlarının tamamı ortadan kalkmışsa: Talep en geç **30 gün içinde** sonuçlandırılır; ilgili kişiye bilgi verilir.
- Veriler üçüncü kişilere aktarılmışsa: Bu durum üçüncü kişiye bildirilir; üçüncü kişi nezdinde Yönetmelik kapsamında işlemler yapılır.
- İşleme şartları tamamen ortadan kalkmamışsa: Talep, KVKK m.13/3 uyarınca gerekçesi açıklanarak reddedilir; ret cevabı 30 gün içinde yazılı veya elektronik olarak iletilir.

Süreç akışı için: bkz. [09-ilgili-kisi-basvurulari/](../09-ilgili-kisi-basvurulari/)

## 12. Politikanın Yayımı ve Güncellenmesi

### 12.1. Yayım

Politika, Yönetim Kurulu onayını takiben:
- Şirket internet sitesinde yayımlanır,
- Şirket içi politika portalında yayımlanır,
- Tüm çalışanlara duyurulur,
- VERBİS kayıt sayfasında atfı yapılır.

### 12.2. Eğitim

- Yeni işe başlayan tüm çalışanlara oryantasyon kapsamında Politika tanıtılır.
- Mevcut çalışanlara yıllık zorunlu KVKK eğitimi verilir; eğitimde Politikanın temel hükümleri işlenir.
- KVKK Sorumlusu, periyodik imha öncesinde ilgili birimlere hatırlatıcı eğitim sunar.

### 12.3. Denetim

- Yıllık iç denetim
- Üç yılda bir bağımsız dış denetim (önerilir)
- Tetiklenmiş denetim (ihlal sonrası, mevzuat değişikliği)

### 12.4. Güncelleme

Politika; mevzuat değişikliği, organizasyonel değişiklik, yeni iş süreci, ihlal kök neden analizi sonucu veya **en azından yıllık** olarak gözden geçirilir. Güncellemeler aşağıdaki sürümleme tablosunda izlenir.
