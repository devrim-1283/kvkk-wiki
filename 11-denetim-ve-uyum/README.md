## English

# 11 — Audit and Compliance

## Purpose

This section defines the audit framework that measures, monitors and continuously improves the Company's compliance with the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 ("KVKK") and its secondary legislation. It is built on the Three Lines of Defence model:

| Line | Role | Responsibility |
|------|------|----------------|
| 1st Line | Process owners (HR, Marketing, IT, Sales etc.) | Day-to-day compliance, control execution, self-assessment |
| 2nd Line | KVKK Officer, Risk Management, Information Security | Policy, monitoring, KPIs, periodic testing |
| 3rd Line | Internal Audit | Independent assurance, audit reporting to the Board |

## Annual Compliance Calendar (Summary)

| Month | Activity | Owner |
|-------|----------|-------|
| January | Annual internal audit plan approval | Internal Audit |
| February | Maturity self-assessment (1st line) | Process owners |
| March | Q4 KPI report to the Board | KVKK Officer |
| April | Periodic destruction cycle | Process owners |
| May | Annual supplier due-diligence | Procurement / KVKK Officer |
| June | Half-year regulatory change impact assessment | Legal |
| July | Annual training refresher | HR / KVKK Officer |
| August | Mid-year DPIA refresh review | KVKK Officer |
| September | Internal audit fieldwork begins | Internal Audit |
| October | Periodic destruction cycle | Process owners |
| November | Maturity score remeasurement, gap closure | KVKK Committee |
| December | Annual audit report presentation to the Board | Internal Audit |

## Output and Evidence Management

All audit outputs (self-assessments, internal audit reports, KPI dashboard screenshots, training records, breach records, periodic destruction minutes) are retained for at least 5 years and stored in a centralised GRC platform so they can be presented immediately during an inspection by the KVKK Authority.

## Türkçe

# 11 — Denetim ve Uyum

## Amaç

Bu bölüm, Şirketin Kişisel Verilerin Korunması Kanunu (KVKK) ve ikincil mevzuat kapsamındaki yükümlülüklerine uyumunu ölçen, izleyen ve sürekli iyileştiren denetim çerçevesini tanımlar. Üç savunma hattı modelini esas alır:

| Hat | Rol | Sorumluluk |
|-----|-----|------------|
| 1. Hat | Süreç sahipleri (İK, Pazarlama, BT, Satış vb.) | Günlük uyum, kontrollerin uygulanması, öz-değerlendirme |
| 2. Hat | KVKK Sorumlusu, Risk Yönetimi, Bilgi Güvenliği | Politika, izleme, KPI, periyodik testler |
| 3. Hat | İç Denetim | Bağımsız güvence, denetim raporu, Yönetim Kuruluna raporlama |
