## 2. Scope (Art. 2)

The Law covers public institutions, public professional bodies, **natural and legal persons** and unincorporated entities that **exist in, operate in or provide services in cyberspace**. Intelligence activities under the police, coast guard and gendarmerie laws, and activities under the MİT law and the Armed Forces Internal Service Law, are excluded. In practice almost every company with an internet presence falls within the scope; how far each duty applies depends on classification by SGB (Art. 6(1)(g)).

Key definitions (Art. 3): *critical infrastructure* (infrastructure hosting IT systems whose compromise could cause loss of life, large-scale economic damage, security gaps or public-order disruption), *cyber incident* (breach of confidentiality, integrity or availability of IT systems or data), *SOME* (cyber incident response team), *vulnerability*, *hosting*.

## 3. Obligations

### 3.1. Everyone using IT systems to provide services or process data (Art. 7(1))

| Item | Duty | Admin. fine if breached (Art. 16(10)) |
|------|------|----------------------------------------|
| (a) | Deliver any data, information, document, hardware, software and other contribution requested by SGB, with priority and on time | TRY 1,000,000 - 10,000,000 (item (a) added by Law 7590) |
| (b) | Take the measures required by legislation for national security, public order or proper delivery of public services; **report vulnerabilities or cyber incidents detected in the area where they provide services to SGB without delay** | TRY 1,000,000 - 10,000,000 |
| (c) | Public bodies and critical infrastructures: procure cyber-security products, systems and services only from experts, producers or companies **authorised and certified by SGB** | TRY 1,000,000 - 10,000,000 |
| (ç) | Cyber-security companies subject to certification: obtain SGB approval **before starting activity** | Operating without required approval is a crime under Art. 16(2) |
| (d) | Comply with SGB policies, strategies, action plans and other regulatory acts on cyber maturity | - |

### 3.2. During audits (Art. 8)

SGB may audit any act within the scope, on site or remotely, through its staff or authorised independent auditors (Art. 8(1)). Audited parties must keep devices, systems, software and hardware open for audit within the given time and keep the needed infrastructure working (Art. 8(4)). Breach: TRY 100,000 - 1,000,000; for commercial companies not less than TRY 100,000 and up to **5% of gross sales** in audited annual financial statements (Art. 16(11)). Searches, copying and seizure in homes, workplaces and closed areas require a judge's decision or, in urgent cases, a prosecutor's written order submitted to the judge within 24 hours (judge decides within 48 hours); in authorised data centres only with a judge's decision; no judge's decision is required for public bodies (Art. 8(5)).

### 3.3. Cyber-security product companies (Art. 18)

- Exports of cyber-security products, systems, software, hardware and services follow procedures set by SGB; products listed as permit-subject need SGB approval (Art. 18(1)).
- Mergers, demergers, share transfers or sales of companies producing such products/services must be **notified** to SGB; transactions giving direct or indirect control or decision power need SGB **approval** (Art. 18(2)).
- Transactions without approval have **no legal validity** (Art. 18(3)).
- Breach of Art. 18 duties: TRY 10,000,000 - 100,000,000 (Art. 16(10)).
- The implementing procedures are to be published by SGB (Art. 18(4)). **None found** on SGB's legislation page on 2026-11-20 (UNVERIFIED that none exist).

## 4. Incident Reporting: Duty and Deadline

- **Statutory duty:** Art. 7(1)(b), report vulnerabilities and cyber incidents "gecikmeksizin" (without delay) to SGB. **The Law contains no hour-based deadline.**
- **Proposed deadlines (draft only):** The draft Cyber Resilience Regulation (SGB, 18.11.2026) would require all parties to report cyber incidents, possible breaches and suspicious situations without delay (draft Art. 27), and organisations obliged to run a Corporate SOME to notify the sectoral SOME (levels 1-2) or the sectoral SOME and SGB (levels 3-5) **within 12 hours of detection**, send an update within **72 hours**, and a detailed report within **one month** (draft Art. 33). Not in force. Source: [draft PDF](https://cdn.siberguvenlik.gov.tr/public/news/siber-dayaniklilik-yonetmeligi.pdf).
- **Older rules still applied:** SOME Communiqué (RG 11.11.2013/28818) Art. 5(6): Corporate SOMEs report incidents "without delay" to USOM and their sectoral SOME; Art. 7(2): sectoral SOMEs report to USOM without delay. Under Law 7545 Prov. Art. 1(6), existing rules not contrary to the Law continue until the new rules enter into force.
- See [olay-bildirim-matrisi.md](olay-bildirim-matrisi.md).

## 5. Data Localisation

- **Law 7545 itself contains no general data-localisation rule.** "Hosting" is defined neutrally (Art. 3(1)(a)); SGB may provide hosting to public bodies (Art. 5(1)(f)); SGB may install products and transfer logs to its own systems (Art. 6(1)(b), (d)).
- Localisation duties exist elsewhere:
  - Presidential Circular 2019/12 (RG 06.07.2019/30823), items 1-3 for public bodies: critical information and data stored securely in Türkiye; public data not kept in cloud storage except private systems or domestic providers under institutional control. (Text read from a secondary reproduction because the Official Gazette PDF is an image: [alomaliye](https://www.alomaliye.com/2019/07/06/bilgi-ve-iletisim-guvenligi-tedbirleri-2019-12/).)
  - Banks: primary and secondary systems must be in Türkiye (BDDK BSEBY Art. 25(1)).
  - Capital-market institutions: primary and secondary systems in Türkiye (SPK VII-128.10 Art. 27(1)).
  - **Draft** Cyber Resilience Regulation Art. 58 (critical infrastructure only): all data, backups, code, logs, metadata and primary/secondary/DR/SOC/NOC sites established and operated in Türkiye; no transfer abroad, no cross-border cloud, no remote access from abroad, save statutory or SGB exceptions. Not in force.
- For KVKK cross-border transfer rules see [07-aktarim/yurtdisi-aktarim-rejimi.md](../07-aktarim/yurtdisi-aktarim-rejimi.md).

## 6. Sanctions

### 6.1. Criminal provisions (Art. 16(1)-(9))

| Para. | Act | Penalty |
|-------|-----|---------|
| (1) | Not giving, or obstructing, information, documents, software, data or hardware requested by authorised bodies or auditors (public bodies excluded) | 1-3 years' imprisonment + 500-1,500 days' judicial fine |
| (2) | Operating without approvals, authorisations or permits required by the Law | 2-4 years + 1,000-2,000 days' judicial fine |
| (3) | Breach of confidentiality duty | 4-8 years |
| (4) | Making available, sharing or selling, without consent, personal data or critical-public-service institutional data previously leaked in cyberspace | 3-5 years |
| (5) | Knowingly creating or spreading false content that a cyber data leak occurred, to cause public concern, fear or panic or to target institutions or persons | 2-5 years |
| (6) | Cyber attack on elements of Türkiye's national power in cyberspace, or keeping data so obtained in cyberspace (unless a heavier crime) | 8-12 years; spreading, sending or selling such data: 10-15 years |
| (7) | Aggravation | +1/3 if by a public official; +1/2 if by more than one person; +1/2 to x2 if within an organisation |
| (8) | Breach of Art. 12 (post-employment bans on former SGB staff, publication ban) | 3-5 years |
| (9) | Abuse of powers under the Law, or causing a data breach by acting contrary to duty in protecting critical infrastructure | 1-3 years |

### 6.2. Administrative fines (Art. 16(10)-(11)) and procedure (Art. 17)

- Art. 7(1)(a)-(c): TRY 1M - 10M. Art. 18: TRY 10M - 100M. Art. 8(4): TRY 100k - 1M or up to 5% of gross sales (commercial companies).
- Defence must be requested first; 30 days to respond (Art. 17(1)). Repeated violations before a decision: one fine increased up to two times; where a benefit or damage arises, the fine is not less than 3x and not more than 5x that amount (Art. 17(2)). Payment within one month; collection under Law 6183 (Art. 17(3)). Appeal to administrative courts (Art. 17(5)).
- Separate regime from Law 7590: SGB may fine TRY 20,000 - 100,000 **per violation** for breaches of its duties under Law 5809 (new Art. 60/A(3)).

## 9. Company Action List

1. Record that the company is within Art. 2 scope; identify whether it sits in one of the 15 critical sectors.
2. Name an SGB liaison and add the SGB KEP address (siberguvenlikbaskanligi@hs01.kep.tr, per SGB FAQ) to the incident contact list.
3. Add "SGB notification under 7545 Art. 7(1)(b)" as a step in the incident runbook next to the KVKK 72-hour step.
4. Prepare a process to answer SGB data requests with priority (Art. 7(1)(a)), with Legal review for confidentiality and KVKK.
5. If the company sells cyber-security products/services: see [siber-sirket-yonetmelikleri.md](siber-sirket-yonetmelikleri.md) (approval before activity, Art. 18 M&A and exports).
6. Track the draft regulations; plan for the 12h/72h/1-month cycle, annual pentest by SGB-authorised bodies and 5-year record retention if adopted as drafted.

## 2. Kapsam (m.2)

Kanun; **siber uzayda varlık gösteren, faaliyet yürüten, hizmet sunan** kamu kurum ve kuruluşlarını, kamu kurumu niteliğindeki meslek kuruluşlarını, **gerçek ve tüzel kişileri** ve tüzel kişiliği bulunmayan kuruluşları kapsar. Polis, sahil güvenlik ve jandarma kanunlarına göre yürütülen istihbari faaliyetler ile MİT Kanunu ve TSK İç Hizmet Kanunu uyarınca yürütülen faaliyetler kapsam dışıdır. Uygulamada internette varlığı olan hemen her şirket kapsamdadır; her yükümlülüğün ne ölçüde uygulanacağı SGB'nin sınıflandırmasına bağlıdır (m.6(1)(g)).

Temel tanımlar (m.3): *kritik altyapı* (işlediği bilgi bozulduğunda can kaybına, büyük ölçekli ekonomik zarara, güvenlik açıklarına veya kamu düzeninin bozulmasına yol açabilecek bilişim sistemlerini barındıran altyapı), *siber olay* (bilişim sistemlerinin veya verinin gizlilik, bütünlük veya erişilebilirliğinin ihlali), *SOME* (siber olaylara müdahale ekibi), *zafiyet*, *barındırma*.

### 3.2. Denetimde (m.8)

SGB kapsamdaki her türlü fiil ve işlemi kendi personeli veya yetkilendirilmiş bağımsız denetçiler eliyle, gerekirse mahallinde denetleyebilir (m.8(1)). Denetlenenler cihaz, sistem, yazılım ve donanımları verilen sürede denetime açık tutmak ve gerekli altyapıyı çalışır vaziyette tutmak zorundadır (m.8(4)). İhlalde: 100.000 - 1.000.000 TL; ticari şirketlerde 100.000 TL'den az olmamak üzere bağımsız denetimden geçmiş yıllık finansal tablolardaki **brüt satış hasılatının %5'ine kadar** (m.16(11)). Konut, işyeri ve kapalı alanlarda arama, kopyalama ve el koyma hâkim kararıyla ya da gecikmesinde sakınca bulunan hâllerde Cumhuriyet savcısının yazılı emriyle yapılır; emir 24 saat içinde hâkim onayına sunulur, hâkim 48 saat içinde karar verir. Yetkilendirilmiş veri merkezlerinde yalnız hâkim kararıyla yapılır; kamu kurumları için hâkim kararı aranmaz (m.8(5)).

### 3.3. Siber güvenlik ürünü üreten şirketler (m.18)

- Siber güvenlik ürün, sistem, yazılım, donanım ve hizmetlerinin yurt dışına satışı SGB'nin belirleyeceği usul ve esaslara göre yapılır; izne tabi ürünlerde SGB onayı alınır (m.18(1)).
- Bu ürünleri üreten şirketlerin birleşme, bölünme, pay devri veya satış işlemleri SGB'ye **bildirilir**; doğrudan veya dolaylı kontrol ya da karar yetkisi sağlayan işlemler SGB **onayına** tabidir (m.18(2)).
- Onaysız işlemler **hukuki geçerlilik kazanmaz** (m.18(3)).
- m.18 yükümlülüklerinin ihlali: 10.000.000 - 100.000.000 TL (m.16(10)).
- Uygulama usul ve esasları SGB yayımlayacaktır (m.18(4)). 2026-11-20'de SGB mevzuat sayfasında **bulunamadı** (hiç olmadığı DOĞRULANMADI).

### 6.2. İdari para cezaları (m.16(10)-(11)) ve usul (m.17)

- m.7(1)(a)-(c): 1 - 10 milyon TL. m.18: 10 - 100 milyon TL. m.8(4): 100 bin - 1 milyon TL veya ticari şirketlerde brüt satış hasılatının %5'ine kadar.
- Önce savunma alınır; tebliğden itibaren 30 gün (m.17(1)). Karar verilinceye kadar birden çok ihlalde tek ceza iki katına kadar artırılır; menfaat veya zarar varsa ceza bunun üç katından az, beş katından fazla olamaz (m.17(2)). Ödeme bir ay içinde; 6183 sayılı Kanuna göre tahsil (m.17(3)). İdari yargı yolu açıktır (m.17(5)).
- 7590 ile gelen ayrı rejim: SGB, 5809 sayılı Kanundaki görevleriyle ilgili yükümlülük ihlallerinde **eylem başına** 20.000 - 100.000 TL idari para cezası verebilir (yeni m.60/A(3)).

## 9. Şirket İçin Eylem Listesi

1. Şirketin m.2 kapsamında olduğunu kayda alın; 15 kritik sektörden birinde olup olmadığını belirleyin.
2. Bir SGB irtibat kişisi atayın; SGB KEP adresini (SGB SSS'ye göre siberguvenlikbaskanligi@hs01.kep.tr) olay iletişim listesine ekleyin.
3. Olay müdahale akışına KVKK 72 saat adımının yanına "7545 m.7(1)(b) uyarınca SGB bildirimi" adımını ekleyin.
4. SGB veri taleplerine öncelikli yanıt verecek, gizlilik ve KVKK açısından Hukuk incelemesinden geçen bir süreç kurun (m.7(1)(a)).
5. Şirket siber güvenlik ürünü veya hizmeti satıyorsa: [siber-sirket-yonetmelikleri.md](siber-sirket-yonetmelikleri.md) (faaliyet öncesi onay, m.18 şirket işlemleri ve ihracat).
6. Taslak yönetmelikleri izleyin; taslak olduğu gibi kabul edilirse 12 saat / 72 saat / 1 ay döngüsünü, SGB yetkili kuruluşlarına yıllık sızma testini ve 5 yıllık kayıt saklamayı planlayın.
