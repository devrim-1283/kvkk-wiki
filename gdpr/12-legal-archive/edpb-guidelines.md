## English

# EDPB Guidelines and former WP29 Reference

The European Data Protection Board (EDPB) — the successor to the Article 29 Working Party (WP29) — publishes guidelines, opinions, and recommendations that interpret the GDPR. While not formally binding (except Article 65 binding decisions), they are highly persuasive and SAs typically follow them. EDPB endorses many WP29 guidelines explicitly.

This reference catalogues the most operationally important guidelines, with adoption year, topic, and link to the wiki section that operationalizes them.

### EDPB binding decisions (Article 65) — selection

Article 65 binding decisions resolve disputes between SAs. Notable examples:

| Decision | Year | Subject | Outcome |
|----------|------|---------|---------|
| 1/2020 | 2020 | Twitter breach (Ireland lead) | First binding decision; clarified breach notification obligations |
| 1/2021 | 2021 | WhatsApp transparency (Ireland lead) | Increased fine and broadened scope |
| 1/2023 | 2023 | Meta IE Facebook lawful basis | Required additional fine and prohibited contractual necessity for personalized ads |
| 2/2022 | 2022 | Instagram children's data | Bound DPC to higher fine |

### Annual reports and EDPB strategy

The EDPB publishes annual reports and multi-year strategies. The 2024-2027 strategy emphasises:

- Enforcement coordination.
- AI and emerging technologies.
- Children's data and vulnerable groups.
- International cooperation.

### Maintenance procedure

The legal team monitors the EDPB website monthly and updates this catalogue within thirty days of new adoption. Each new entry triggers an internal change-impact assessment with an action item, owner, and target date.

### Citation conventions

Cite as: "EDPB Guidelines [Number]/[Year] on [topic], adopted [date], version [version]." For WP29: "Article 29 Working Party, [WP-number] [topic], adopted [date]."
