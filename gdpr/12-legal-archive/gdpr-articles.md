# GDPR Articles 1-99 — Summary, Operational Impact, Wiki Cross-Link

This reference summarizes every article of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 in plain language, identifies operational impact, and links to the wiki section that translates the obligation into practice. Group headers follow the chapter structure of the regulation.

| Art | Title | Operational impact | Wiki link |
|-----|-------|-------------------|-----------|
| 1 | Subject-matter and objectives | Establishes scope: protection of natural persons in processing of personal data and free movement | `01-core-concepts/scope.md` |
| 2 | Material scope | Wholly or partly automated processing; non-automated in filing systems; exclusions: household, law enforcement directive 2016/680 | `01-core-concepts/scope.md` |
| 3 | Territorial scope | Establishment in EU; offering goods/services to data subjects in EU; monitoring behavior in EU | `01-core-concepts/territorial-scope.md` |

### Recitals reference

Although not legally binding in their own right, recitals (171 in total) are essential interpretive aids. Key recitals to know:

- Recitals 1-4: fundamental rights basis.
- Recital 26: pseudonymisation, anonymisation.
- Recitals 32, 42, 43: consent.
- Recital 47: legitimate interests, reasonable expectations.
- Recitals 51-56: special categories.
- Recital 75: risk-based approach examples.
- Recital 91: high-risk processing.
- Recital 173: relationship with ePrivacy.
