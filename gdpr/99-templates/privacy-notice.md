## English

# Privacy Notice Templates

Three notices below: (A) customer / website notice, (B) employee notice, (C) CCTV notice. All comply with Article 12 (transparent, intelligible, accessible, free) and Article 13 (information collected from the data subject).

Use the layered approach recommended by EDPB Guidelines on Transparency: a short summary at the top, followed by full detail. Each template ends with mandatory information. Tailor where indicated.

---
