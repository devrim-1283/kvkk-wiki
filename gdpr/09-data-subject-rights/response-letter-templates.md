## English

### How to use these templates

Each template covers the substantive response. They are drafted in plain language, are bilingual where the requester writes in Turkish, and are produced from the case management system with case-specific data filled by automation. The DPO sign-off applies to template changes; per-case sign-off applies to the populated letter. Square brackets `[…]` mark fields filled by the system or handler.

Common preamble (used for every right):

> Dear [Salutation Name],
>
> Thank you for contacting us. We received your request on [Date] under reference [Case ID]. This is our substantive response under Article [N] GDPR (and where applicable, KVKK Article 11).

Common closing (used for every right):

> If you would like to discuss this response, our DPO can be reached at [DPO email] or [DPO phone].
>
> You have the right to lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority — [Lead SA name and contact] — and the right to seek a judicial remedy. If you are resident in Türkiye, you may also contact the Kişisel Verileri Koruma Kurumu (KVKK) at https://www.kvkk.gov.tr.
>
> Yours sincerely,
> [DPO name and signature]
> Data Protection Officer, [Controller legal name]

### 1. Article 13 / 14 — Information letter (post-collection clarification)

Used when a data subject asks for information that should have been provided at collection but the requester wants reconfirmation, or when collection occurred indirectly (Article 14).

> Below is the information about how we process your personal data:
>
> 1. **Identity and contact** of the controller: [Controller legal name, registered address, registration number, DPO email].
> 2. **Purposes** of processing: [list].
> 3. **Lawful basis** for each purpose (Article 6, and Article 9 where applicable): [list].
> 4. **Where we obtained your data** (for indirect collection): [list of sources].
> 5. **Categories of personal data** we process about you: [list].
> 6. **Recipients or categories of recipients**: [list].
> 7. **Transfers outside the EEA**, with safeguards: [list with Article 46 mechanism, e.g. SCCs, adequacy decision].
> 8. **Retention period** or criteria: [per category].
> 9. **Your rights**: access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection, withdrawal of consent, complaint to supervisory authority.
> 10. **Existence of automated decision-making** including profiling: [yes/no, with logic explanation if yes].

### 3. Article 16 — Rectification

#### Granted

> Thank you for your request. We have updated the following information in our systems:
>
> [Field] — was: [old], now: [new]. Update applied on [date], in [systems].
>
> Where Article 19 applies, we have notified the following recipients of your rectification: [list, or "we determined this would involve disproportionate effort, see below"]. The full list of recipients is available on request.

#### Refused (where data is in fact accurate)

> We have reviewed the data you asked us to rectify. Based on [evidence — invoice issued at the time, source document, identity record], we believe the data is accurate. We have therefore not changed it. If you have evidence we have not seen, please share it and we will reconsider.
>
> You may also exercise your right to restriction under Article 18(1)(a) while accuracy is contested.

### 4. Article 17 — Erasure

#### Granted

>
> [List of categories or systems.]
>
> Erasure was completed on [date]. Some data has been retained as detailed below, where Article 17(3) exemptions apply:
>
> [List, e.g.: "We must retain accounting records for 8 years under tax law (Article 17(3)(b)). We have set those records to a restricted-access state and they will be deleted on [date]."]
>

#### Refused

> We are unable to erase your data because [Article 17(3) ground — e.g. (a) freedom of expression and information; (b) compliance with a legal obligation; (c) public interest in public health; (d) archiving in the public interest, scientific or historical research, statistics; (e) establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims].
>

>
> We have reviewed the request carefully. If your circumstances change, please contact us again.

#### Partial

>

### 7. Article 21 — Objection

#### Direct marketing

> We have stopped using your personal data for direct marketing purposes effective [date]. This includes [channels: email, SMS, postal, online advertising, social-media targeting, etc.].
>
> You will continue to receive transactional and service-related communications that are necessary for our contract with you (e.g. order confirmations, security alerts).

#### Other processing under legitimate interest or public task

> We have considered your objection. [Either: We have stopped the processing as you requested. / Or: We continue the processing because we have demonstrated compelling legitimate grounds that override your interests, rights and freedoms — specifically [reason], or because the processing is for the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims.]
>
> If we continue, we are happy to discuss the reasoning further.

### 8. Article 22 — Automated decision-making

> Thank you for your request relating to the [decision name / type] made on [date].
>
> [Where Article 22(1) applies and we made a decision based solely on automated processing producing legal or similarly significant effect:]
>
> The logic involved is, in summary: [meaningful information about the logic per Article 13(2)(f) / 14(2)(g) / 15(1)(h)]. The significance and envisaged consequences are: [explanation].
>
> You have the right to obtain human intervention, to express your point of view, and to contest the decision. We have arranged for [name, role] to review your case and contact you by [date]. If you wish to provide additional information for that review, please reply to this letter.

### 9. Refusal under Article 12(5)

> We have considered your request and concluded that it is [manifestly unfounded / excessive] for the following reasons: [specific facts of the case — e.g. repetitive nature within short period, identical request already answered on date X, nature of request].
>
> Under Article 12(5), in such circumstances we may [refuse to act / charge a reasonable fee of [amount] reflecting our administrative costs]. We have decided to [decision].
>
> If you believe this decision is wrong, you may complain to [Lead SA] and you may seek a judicial remedy.

### 10. Partial response (any right)

> Your request relates to several elements. We have addressed each as follows:
>
> | Element | Outcome | Reason |
> |---------|---------|--------|
> | [Element 1] | Granted | — |
> | [Element 2] | Refused | [Article ground] |
> | [Element 3] | Restricted | [Article ground] |

### 11. Article 12(3) extension notice

> Thank you for your request received on [date]. We have determined that, given [the complexity of your request / the number of requests we have received that relate to similar systems / the volume of records we must search], we will not be able to provide a substantive response within one month.
>
> We are extending the deadline by a further two months, to [new date], under Article 12(3) GDPR. We will provide our substantive response on or before that date. We will continue to give your request priority.

### 12. Identity verification request

> Thank you for your request received on [date]. To protect your data, we need to verify your identity before we proceed.
>
> Please provide [specific item — e.g. confirm your reply from the email address on file, or provide the last four digits of your customer ID and the month of your most recent invoice].
>
> We will only ask for what is necessary and proportionate. We will use the information only to verify your identity for this request and we will delete it once verification is complete.
>
> The one-month deadline starts when we have completed verification.

### 13. Article 11 response (we cannot identify you)

> Thank you for your request received on [date]. Having reviewed our records, we have determined that the data we hold cannot be linked to a natural person without further information, and we cannot link your request to a specific record on the basis of what you have provided.
>
> Under Article 11(2) GDPR, we are not obliged to obtain or maintain additional information solely for the purpose of complying with the rights in Articles 15 to 20. However, if you can provide any of the following [specific identifiers], we will reconsider: [list].
>
> If you have no further information to provide, the rights in Articles 15 to 20 do not apply in this case. The right to lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority remains.

>
> Bizimle iletişime geçtiğiniz için teşekkür ederiz. Talebinizi [Tarih] tarihinde [Vaka Kimliği] referansıyla aldık. Bu, GDPR Madde [N] (ve uygulanabilir olduğunda KVKK Madde 11) kapsamındaki esaslı yanıtımızdır.

Ortak kapanış:

> Bu yanıtı tartışmak isterseniz, VKK Sorumlumuza [VKK e-posta] veya [VKK telefon] üzerinden ulaşabilirsiniz.
>
> Denetim makamı — [Baş DM adı ve iletişim] — nezdinde şikayette bulunma ve adli çözüm arama hakkınız bulunmaktadır. Türkiye'de ikamet ediyorsanız, Kişisel Verileri Koruma Kurumu'na (KVKK) https://www.kvkk.gov.tr adresinden de başvurabilirsiniz.
