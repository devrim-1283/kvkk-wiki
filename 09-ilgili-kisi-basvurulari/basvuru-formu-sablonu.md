> "My employment was terminated on 2024-12-31. Pursuant to KVKK Art. 11(e), I request erasure of all personal data outside SGK and Tax Procedure Law retention obligations - in particular additional candidate documents, performance evaluations, and internal survey responses..."

> "My address in the company system is wrong. The correct one is: [new address]. Pursuant to KVKK Art. 11(d) and Art. 11(f), I request rectification using the attached residence document and notification of the courier company to which my data was previously transferred..."

## POST-APPLICATION PROCESS

1. When your application is received, an **automatic acknowledgment** with a **reference number** is sent within 24 hours.
2. If identity verification is required, you may be asked for additional information/documents.
3. Pursuant to KVKK Art. 13, a response is sent within **at most 30 days**.
4. If you find our response insufficient or your request is refused, you have the right to file a complaint with the Personal Data Protection Authority **within 30 days** (KVKK Art. 14).

## BAŞVURU SONRASI SÜREÇ

1. Başvurunuz alındığında 24 saat içinde **otomatik onay** ve **referans numarası** tarafınıza iletilir.
2. Kimlik doğrulama gerekiyorsa size ek bilgi/belge sorulur.
3. KVKK m.13 gereği **en geç 30 gün** içinde tarafınıza cevap verilir.
4. Cevabımızı yetersiz bulmanız veya talebinizin reddedilmesi halinde **30 gün içinde** Kişisel Verileri Koruma Kurulu'na şikayette bulunma hakkınız saklıdır (KVKK m.14).
