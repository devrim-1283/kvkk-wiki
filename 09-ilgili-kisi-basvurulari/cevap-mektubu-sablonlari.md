## English

# Response Letter Templates

> All templates contain the mandatory elements of **Communiqué Art. 6(4)**:
> (a) Information about the data controller / representative,
> (b) Applicant's: name and surname, T.R. ID, nationality, passport, address, e-mail, telephone, fax,
> (c) Subject of the request,
> (ç) The data controller's explanations regarding the application.

> The response is delivered in **written or electronic** form (Art. 6(3)).

## ANNEX 1 - Considerations for Online Responses

- Delivery via KEP is legally equivalent to written notification.
- Send the encrypted PDF password over a **separate channel** (SMS, telephone).
- For e-mail dispatch, request "read receipt" + "delivery confirmation".
- The response letter is produced in PDF on company letterhead quality.

## ANNEX 2 - Template Management

- All templates are approved by Legal Counsel.
- Annual update + tracking of Communiqué changes.
- Version control (Git).
- **Personalization is mandatory** when using the template - copy-paste is not accepted.
